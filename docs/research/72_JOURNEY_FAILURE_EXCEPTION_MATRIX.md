## 1. Overview & Resiliency Mandate

Clinical environments across the Indian public health system operate under severe infrastructure, human, and logistical stress. Systems that require continuous cloud connectivity, perfect data inputs, or frictionless hardware inevitably fail at the bedside, endangering human life.

CLINOVA AI establishes an **Exhaustive Failure & Exception Architecture** covering **31 discrete failure modes** across six critical operational domains:
1. Infrastructure & Hardware Volatility (Failures 1–5)
2. Identity, Linkage & Data Governance (Failures 6–10)
3. Multimodal Ingestion & Inaccurate Evidence (Failures 11–15)
4. Resource Exhaustion & Logistics Deficits (Failures 16–21)
5. Clinical Staffing & Human Behavioral Exceptions (Failures 22–27)
6. Data Synchronization & Disaster Recovery (Failures 28–31)

## 2. Exhaustive 31-Scenario Failure Matrix

| # | Failure Mode | Current State | Clinical & Safety Risk | Automated System Response | Required Human Response | Recovery State | Mandatory Audit Event |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **No Internet / WAN Outage** | Any State | Inability to access cloud APIs or sync with central ABDM registry. | Activates **100% Offline Local LAN Mode**; routes all SLM/OCR to edge Mini-PC; queues sync packets in IndexedDB/SQLite. | Health worker continues clinical intake without interruption. | Same State (Offline) | `OFFLINE_MODE_ENGAGED` |
| **02** | **Local LAN Failure** | `S02`–`S12` | Inability for triage nurse terminal to communicate with doctor terminal over Wi-Fi/Ethernet. | Devices switch to **Peer-to-Peer Encrypted QR / Wi-Fi Direct Mode**; nurse terminal displays high-density 2D barcode of case dossier. | Doctor scans nurse screen QR code using webcam/tablet to ingest Master Case. | Same State (Local P2P) | `P2P_FALLBACK_ENGAGED` |
| **03** | **Local Server / Mini-PC Crash** | `S02`–`S20` | Loss of running session and active queue memory. | Watchdog daemon executes instant process restart ($< 8$s); reloads database from WAL journal; restores in-memory queue. | Staff re-authenticates; uncommitted draft loaded from browser local storage. | State at last checkpoint | `NODE_CRASH_RECOVERED` |
| **04** | **Device Hardware Failure (Tablet Drops)** | `S02` / `S07` | Hardware damage to intake tablet at bedside. | Session state resides in local LAN server memory; zero data lost on broken tablet. | Worker picks up spare facility tablet, scans patient synthetic wristband; session resumes. | `STATE_STAFF_DATA_PENDING` | `DEVICE_FAILOVER_LOGGED` |
| **05** | **Power Outage / Grid Collapse** | Any State | Sudden blackout shutting down desktops and network switches. | Hardware runs on uninterruptible power supply (UPS) / 12V DC battery bank; triggers low-power UI mode. | Staff continues triage on battery-powered mobile tablets and laptops. | Same State | `POWER_FAILOVER_ENGAGED` |
| **06** | **Duplicate Case Creation** | `S01` / `S07` | Two triage desks inadvertently create separate cases for the same patient. | Real-time deduplication engine flags identical synthetic patient demographic tokens and active queue collision. | Triage supervisor confirms duplicate; system merges secondary records into Master Case. | `STATE_CONSOLIDATED` | `DUPLICATE_CASE_MERGED` |
| **07** | **Wrong Patient Identification** | `S11` / `S12` | Doctor opens Case A but physically evaluates Patient B in busy OPD. | Doctor verifies synthetic token and photo/demographics before clinical sign-off. | Doctor clicks "Wrong Patient Mismatch"; system returns Case A to queue; loads Patient B. | `STATE_DOCTOR_QUEUED` | `PATIENT_MISMATCH_CORRECTED`|
| **08** | **Unknown Identity (Unconscious / Trauma)** | `S01` / `S22` | Patient unable to provide name, age, or identity. | Allocates ephemeral **Anonymous Emergency Token** (`EMG-YYYYMMDD-XXXX`); defaults consent to `EMERGENCY_IMPLIED`. | Emergency casualty team begins resuscitation immediately without administrative delay. | `STATE_EMERGENCY_ACTIVE` | `ANONYMOUS_TOKEN_ISSUED` |
| **09** | **Identity Discovered Later** | `S22` / `S19` | Family arrives with ID card 3 hours after anonymous resuscitation. | Executes **Cryptographic Identity Bind**; attaches permanent Aadhaar/ABHA ID to existing Master Case. | Administrator verifies family identity proof; signs identity reconciliation form. | `STATE_WARD_ADMITTED` | `IDENTITY_RETROACTIVELY_BOUND`|
| **10** | **Accidental Merge Attempt** | `S07` / `S12` | Staff attempts to merge two patients with similar names. | Cryptographic Merkle tree rejects merge if demographic hash mismatch exceeds safety threshold. | Supervisor review required; merge aborted; logs security warning. | Same State | `MERGE_ATTEMPT_REJECTED` |
| **11** | **Missing Vitals at Intake** | `S05` | Inability to score risk safely without objective physiological metrics. | Information Sufficiency Gate calculates $S < 0.65$; automatically diverts case to Nurse Worklist. | Frontline triage nurse physically measures BP, SpO2, HR, RR with calibrated instruments. | `STATE_STAFF_DATA_PENDING` | `INSUFFICIENT_VITALS_DIVERT`|
| **12** | **Missing Prior Documents** | `S02` | Patient forgets old prescriptions or discharge papers at home. | System marks historical entities as `UNKNOWN`; generates targeted questions for current symptoms. | Patient/ASHA verbally narrates drug history; nurse flags uncertainty on review. | `STATE_MISSING_AUDIT` | `MISSING_DOCS_RECORDED` |
| **13** | **Incorrect / Corrupted OCR Extraction** | `S03` / `S04` | Blurred photo leads to misread lab value (e.g., Hb 1.2 instead of 12.0). | Sets extraction confidence $< 0.40$; highlights field in red on Visual Review Screen. | Reviewer or nurse inspects raw crop, edits value to 12.0, and sets provenance to `USER_EDITED`. | `STATE_EXTRACTION_REVIEW` | `OCR_CORRECTION_LOGGED` |
| **14** | **Incorrect Speech Transcription** | `S03` / `S04` | Dialect phonetics misinterpret spoken complaint (e.g., "chhati" chest vs "chhati" umbrella). | Side-by-side snippet review displays audio player alongside extracted text; confidence score flagged. | Frontline health worker or patient listens to audio snippet and corrects chief complaint. | `STATE_EXTRACTION_REVIEW` | `TRANSCRIPTION_CORRECTED` |
| **15** | **Incorrect Vernacular Translation** | `S03` / `S04` | Local clinical idiom lost in automated English normalization. | Retains native vernacular transcript permanently alongside translated clinical terms. | Attending doctor (bilingual) reviews original Odia/Hindi audio snippet if ambiguity arises. | `STATE_DOCTOR_REVIEWING` | `TRANSLATION_OVERRIDDEN` |
| **16** | **Conflicting Evidence Across Sources** | `S05` / `S12` | Patient states fever for 2 days; clinic slip shows fever for 2 weeks. | Flags parameter with high-contrast yellow `CONFLICTING` badge; prevents auto-consolidation. | Doctor evaluates conflict during physical history; verifies authoritative clinical timeline. | `STATE_DOCTOR_REVIEWING` | `EVIDENCE_CONFLICT_ADJUDICATED`|
| **17** | **Stale Facility Capability Data** | `S14` / `S20` | Receiving hospital bed telemetry is $> 12\text{ hours}$ old. | Displays amber `STALE TELEMETRY` warning banner; penalizes facility score in ranking. | Referral desk coordinator executes mandatory telephonic confirmation before ambulance departure. | `STATE_REFERRAL_PENDING` | `STALE_CAPABILITY_OVERRIDE` |
| **18** | **No Specialist Available On-Site** | `S14` / `S15` | Patient requires emergency orthopedic surgery; on-site surgeon on leave. | Feasibility Index set to $\Phi_{\text{local}} = 0.0$; Orchestration Engine triggers **Referral Advisory**. | Doctor initiates inter-facility transfer to nearest district hospital with on-duty orthopedist. | `STATE_REFERRAL_PENDING` | `SPECIALIST_DEFICIT_TRIGGER` |
| **19** | **No Diagnostic Capability (X-Ray Down)** | `S14` / `S15` | Local X-ray tube burned out; patient has suspected hip fracture. | Resource registry logs diagnostic downtime; flags feasibility deficit for orthopedic pathway. | Doctor splints limb, administers analgesia, and initiates transfer or outpatient voucher. | `STATE_REFERRAL_PENDING` | `DIAGNOSTIC_DOWNTIME_LOGGED` |
| **20** | **No Inpatient Bed Available (Ward Full)** | `S18` | Inpatient medical ward operating at 100% capacity. | Blocks automated bed assignment; alerts doctor: *"Ward full (24/24 beds occupied)"*. | Doctor reviews alternatives: Step-Down observation bay, temporary surge cot, or transfer. | `STATE_WARD_REQUESTED` | `WARD_SATURATION_BLOCKED` |
| **21** | **Referral Rejected by Destination** | `S20` | Receiving hospital reports sudden casualty saturation upon inquiry. | Rerouting Engine removes facility from candidate pool; computes next-best facility in corridor. | Doctor approves alternate destination hospital within 90 seconds. | `STATE_REFERRAL_PENDING` | `REFERRAL_REJECTION_REROUTED`|
| **22** | **Transport Failure (Ambulance Breakdown)** | `S21` | Dispatched ambulance suffers flat tire / mechanical breakdown en route. | Paramedic triggers **Transport SOS Alert**; notifies central 108 dispatch and originating hospital. | Central dispatch routes nearest mutual-aid ambulance; paramedic monitors vitals. | `STATE_TRANSFER_IN_TRANSIT` | `TRANSPORT_BREAKDOWN_SOS` |
| **23** | **Patient Refusal / AMA** | `S15`–`S20` | Family refuses recommended hospital admission or tertiary transfer. | Generates structured vernacular **High-Risk Treatment Refusal Form**; locks legal documentation. | Clinician counsels family on fatal risks; obtains witness signatures; prescribes best oral therapy. | `STATE_ROUTINE_CARE` / `CLOSED` | `PATIENT_AMA_LOGGED` |
| **24** | **Consent Unavailable (Unconscious Solo)** | `S01` | Patient in coma, no family, life-threatening emergency. | Enforces statutory **Emergency Implied Consent Waiver** under Indian law and NMC regulations. | Emergency physician and casualty medical officer document emergency necessity in notes. | `STATE_EMERGENCY_ACTIVE` | `EMERGENCY_CONSENT_WAIVED` |
| **25** | **Doctor Unavailable (OPD Overload)** | `S11` | Doctor delayed in emergency surgery; queue wait times exceed 90 mins. | Dynamic Queue decays priority over time; alerts triage nurse of prolonged wait thresholds. | Triage nurse conducts repeat bedside vitals checks in waiting area to detect silent deterioration. | `STATE_DOCTOR_QUEUED` | `QUEUE_OVERFLOW_ALERTED` |
| **26** | **Nurse Unavailable at Triage Desk** | `S07` | Frontline nurse summoned to manage casualty crash. | Triage worklist sounds intermittent priority chime; flags overflow to medical officer. | Doctor performs on-site point-of-care vitals during consultation, bypassing staff desk. | `STATE_DOCTOR_REVIEWING` | `STAFF_BYPASS_VERIFIED` |
| **27** | **Emergency Escalation During Routine Intake** | `S02`–`S06` | Patient in waiting room or at intake console suddenly collapses. | **Break-Glass Emergency Protocol:** Any staff member taps emergency button; freezes routine UI. | Crash team arrives; patient rolled to resuscitation bay; 30s ABCD vitals initiated. | `STATE_EMERGENCY_ACTIVE` | `MID_INTAKE_EMERGENCY_TRIGGER`|
| **28** | **Patient Deterioration While Waiting** | `S11` | Patient waiting in OPD queue develops chest pain and diaphoresis. | Queue monitoring alerts staff; nurse presses bedside emergency escalate button. | Case instantly promoted to `PRIORITY_CRITICAL_P1`; patient wheeled directly into casualty. | `STATE_EMERGENCY_ACTIVE` | `WAITING_ROOM_DETERIORATION` |
| **29** | **Patient Leaves Before Completion (Walkout)** | `S07` / `S11` | Frustrated patient walks out of hospital without seeing doctor. | Inactivity timer (180 mins) prompts nurse verification call; updates status to `PATIENT_WALKOUT`. | Nurse attempts telephonic check; if high-risk, notifies community health worker (ASHA). | `STATE_CLOSED` | `PATIENT_WALKOUT_RECORDED` |
| **30** | **Concurrent Sync Collision** | `S08` / `S12` | Nurse and doctor edit vitals and notes simultaneously on separate PCs. | Optimistic concurrency control evaluates version vector; merges non-overlapping fields safely. | If conflicting field detected, prompts doctor with side-by-side reconciliation screen. | `STATE_CLINICIAN_VERIFIED` | `SYNC_CONFLICT_RESOLVED` |
| **31** | **Crash Recovery / State Restoration** | Any State | Workstation suddenly loses power during clinical documentation. | On reboot, local IndexedDB / SQLite journal reconstructs uncommitted text within 2 seconds. | Doctor sees prompt: *"Restore uncommitted consultation notes for PT-9821?"* Clicks Restore. | Resumed State | `SESSION_STATE_RESTORED` |
