## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Methodology

This master comparison table benchmarks CLINOVA AI against 18 global and Indian systems spanning clinical triage, ambient documentation, closed-loop referral, hospital capacity management, public health surveillance, and enterprise EHRs.

The comparison adheres strictly to factual technical evidence across the **27 mandatory audit dimensions** specified by the Phase 2 charter. It rejects marketing claims and evaluates deployed capabilities, architectural constraints, and documented clinical limitations.

## 3. Key Takeaways from the Competitor Audit

1. **No System Unites the Triad:** Enterprise software excels in silos: Epic and Abridge excel at clinical documentation; TeleTracking and LeanTaaS excel at bed capacity; IHIP and ESSENCE excel at public surveillance. **No system bridges all three into a single frontline care decision engine.**
2. **The Economic Chasm:** Commercial solutions require subscription fees ($100k–$1M+ annually) that are utterly unviable for public health departments in India.
3. **The Vulnerability of Generative AI:** Babylon Health proved that deploying generative chat without rigid deterministic bounds leads to catastrophic diagnostic failure. CLINOVA's deterministic rules (`TRIAGE-R01` to `TRIAGE-R06`) provide a vital safety barrier.
