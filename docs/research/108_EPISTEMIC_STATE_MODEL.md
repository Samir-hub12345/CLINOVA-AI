## 1. Epistemic Architecture: Beyond Binary Clinical Truth

In conventional hospital databases, clinical fields exist in one of two states: `NULL` (absent) or populated (treated as universally true). This binary oversimplification creates catastrophic clinical vulnerabilities:
- An unmeasured blood pressure is mistaken for normal ($120/80\text{ mmHg}$ imputed by default).
- A wildly degraded OCR read of a lab slip is treated with the same weight as a physical serum blood draw.
- Two contradictory measurements (e.g. nurse cuff $190/110$ vs patient self-statement $120/80$) overwrite each other, obliterating the conflict.
- Algorithmic inferences from language models are presented as confirmed diagnoses.

**CLINOVA AI Epistemic Doctrine:**
Clinical knowledge is intrinsically multi-valued, probabilistic, and evolving. A clinical parameter must be governed by an explicit **Epistemic State Machine** that formalizes its truth value, its transition rules, and its downstream clinical eligibility.

## 2. The Six Canonical Epistemic States

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       SIX CANONICAL EPISTEMIC STATES                        │
├─────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Epistemic State │ Formal Epistemic Definition                               │
├─────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ KNOWN           │ High-confidence, corroborated parameter actively present. │
│ UNKNOWN         │ Explicitly missing or unmeasured; zero-imputation enforced│
│ CONFLICTING     │ Two or more discordant values coexist across sources.     │
│ UNRELIABLE      │ Measured/extracted but degraded, uncalibrated, or low-conf│
│ VERIFIED        │ Legally attested by an authenticated human clinician (RMP)│
│ INFERRED        │ Algorithmic hypothesis generated by SLM, LLM, or rules.   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Detailed Epistemic State Specifications

### 3.1 `KNOWN`
- **Creation Condition:** Instantiated when a clinical datum is captured with high source confidence ($C \ge 0.85$), uncompromised media quality ($Q \ge 0.80$), and without contradictory evidence in the current case context. Typically generated by calibrated nursing vital sign entry (`STAFF_ENTERED`), clear audio transcription confirmed by staff, or high-fidelity OCR verified by a medical clerk.
- **Allowed Transitions:** 
  - $\to \text{VERIFIED}$: When signed off by an RMP.
  - $\to \text{CONFLICTING}$: If a new contradictory evidence record arrives.
  - $\to \text{UNRELIABLE}$: If subsequent sensor telemetry indicates the device was malfunctioning.
- **Authority to Change:** Triage Nurse, Medical Officer, Attending Clinician, or System Conflict Detector.
- **Required Evidence:** Valid raw value, normalized unit, calibrated confidence metric, and actor attribution.
- **Downstream Effects:** Fully eligible for physiological risk scoring (NEWS2, Shock Index), symptom trajectory analysis, and triage queue sorting.

---

### 3.2 `UNKNOWN`
- **Creation Condition:** Instantiated when a clinical parameter required by clinical guidelines or triage protocols has NOT been measured or captured. Enforces the **Zero-Imputation Law** (Phase 6 `INV-02`): missing data is stored explicitly with value `NULL` or sentinel `UNKNOWN`, never filled with default population averages.
- **Allowed Transitions:** 
  - $\to \text{KNOWN}$: When a valid measurement is entered by staff.
  - $\to \text{INFERRED}$: When an AI model projects an advisory estimate based on correlated findings.
  - $\to \text{UNRELIABLE}$: When an attempted measurement yields a degraded or illegible result.
- **Authority to Change:** System intake audit engine, Triage Nurse, or Clinician upon data acquisition.
- **Required Evidence:** Parameter domain, canonical LOINC/SNOMED code, clinical importance tier ($T_1, T_2, T_3$).
- **Downstream Effects:** Lowers the Information Sufficiency Score $S = \sum w_i I_i / \sum w_i$. If Tier 1 critical vitals (SpO2, SBP, HR) are `UNKNOWN`, the case is hard-blocked from doctor queue promotion and diverted to the Nurse Missing-Data Worklist.

---

### 3.3 `CONFLICTING`
- **Creation Condition:** Automatically instantiated by the System Conflict Detector when two or more active evidence records for the exact same clinical concept (e.g. Systolic Blood Pressure) disagree beyond a defined clinical delta $\epsilon$ (e.g. $|\Delta\text{SBP}| > 20\text{ mmHg}$) within the same temporal epoch.
- **Allowed Transitions:** 
  - $\to \text{VERIFIED}$: When an authenticated clinician resolves the conflict by designating the active physiological value or documenting a valid clinical reconciliation.
  - $\to \text{KNOWN}$: If one of the conflicting records is formally retracted or marked invalid due to hardware error.
- **Authority to Change:** Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP) ONLY. Frontline nurses or AI agents CANNOT resolve conflicts.
- **Required Evidence:** Both competing `evidence_record_id` pointers, the detected variance delta, and the conflict detection timestamp.
- **Downstream Effects:** Visually highlighted in bright amber/red on the clinician review dashboard. Algorithmic risk engines adopt the **Safety-Pessimistic Principle**: the highest-acuity value acts as a protective tripwire for emergency triage without declaring it medically verified.

---

### 3.4 `UNRELIABLE`
- **Creation Condition:** Instantiated when a datum is captured from a degraded or questionable source: low OCR extraction confidence ($C < 0.70$), smudged document image ($Q < 0.50$), acoustic recording with SNR $< 10\text{ dB}$, uncalibrated home sensor, or patient report characterized by severe cognitive confusion or intoxication.
- **Allowed Transitions:** 
  - $\to \text{KNOWN}$: If corroborated by an objective physical re-measurement.
  - $\to \text{UNKNOWN}$: If rejected entirely by clinical staff and scheduled for fresh capture.
- **Authority to Change:** Staff Nurse, Medical Officer, or Attending Clinician.
- **Required Evidence:** Quality metrics (SNR, blur metric, OCR character confidence), reason for degradation.
- **Downstream Effects:** Excluded from baseline physiological risk scoring. Triggers an automated prompt on the Nurse Worklist: *"SpO2 reading unreliable due to poor plethysmograph waveform — please repeat manually."*

---

### 3.5 `VERIFIED`
- **Creation Condition:** Instantiated exclusively when a Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP) reviews, attests, and digitally signs off on a clinical fact, examination sign, diagnosis, or disposition on the Doctor Workbench.
- **Allowed Transitions:** 
  - $\to \text{CONFLICTING}$: If new contradictory post-verification diagnostic data arrives (e.g., an unexpected lab result contradicting an initial clinical impression).
  - Terminal in routine flows; can be amended via non-destructive retrospective addendum.
- **Authority to Change:** Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP) holding a valid National Medical Register (NMR) credential.
- **Required Evidence:** Clinician User UUID, NMR registration number, verification timestamp, and cryptographic signature hash.
- **Downstream Effects:** Medico-legally binding under Indian law (NMC Regulations 2023, BSA 2023). Unlocks definitive clinical actions: drug prescriptions, ward admissions, operative authorizations, and final discharge summaries.

---

### 3.6 `INFERRED`
- **Creation Condition:** Instantiated whenever a clinical finding, differential diagnosis hypothesis, symptom progression trajectory, or risk category is generated by an artificial intelligence model (LLM/SLM) or heuristic predictive model.
- **Allowed Transitions:** 
  - $\to \text{VERIFIED}$: ONLY upon explicit clinician acceptance and sign-off.
  - $\to \text{REJECTED}$: Clinician explicitly dismisses the AI hypothesis.
  - $\to \text{CONFLICTING}$: When an AI deduction contradicts verified bedside clinical findings.
- **Authority to Change:** Clinician or Triage Nurse.
- **Required Evidence:** Model architecture, prompt version, confidence calibration, input evidence linkage.
- **Downstream Effects:** Advisory decision support ONLY. Clearly branded with mandatory amber advisory markers. Completely prohibited from executing autonomous actions.

## 4. The Cardinal Law: $\text{INFERRED} \neq \text{VERIFIED}$

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 THE CARDINAL LAW OF CLINICAL AI SAFETY                      │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                             │
│                        INFERRED  ≠  VERIFIED                                │
│                                                                             │
│   Under no operational, technical, emergency, or offline circumstances     │
│   can an AI-generated inference, model output, or automated deduction      │
│   transition to the VERIFIED state autonomously or implicitly.              │
│                                                                             │
│   VERIFIED requires an explicit, authenticated, affirmative clinical        │
│   attestation by a licensed Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP).          │
│                                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Mathematical Formulation
Let $\mathcal{E}$ be the set of all evidence records in case $\mathcal{C}$. Let $\text{Actor}(a)$ denote the entity initiating a state transition, where $\text{Role}(a) \in \{\text{AI\_AGENT}, \text{PATIENT}, \text{NURSE}, \text{RMP}\}$.
$$\forall e \in \mathcal{E}, \quad \left(e.\text{epistemic\_status} \to \text{'VERIFIED'}\right) \implies \left(\text{Role}(\text{Actor}) = \text{'RMP'} \land \text{Actor.nmr\_number} \neq \text{NULL}\right)$$
$$\forall a \text{ where } \text{Role}(a) = \text{'AI\_AGENT'}, \quad \mathcal{T}(e.\text{epistemic\_status} \xrightarrow{a} \text{'VERIFIED'}) = \mathbf{\bot} \quad (\text{Strictly Forbidden})$$

## 6. Coexistence and Epistemic Pluralism

In CLINOVA AI, **multiple evidence records for the same clinical concept CAN and MUST coexist** across different epistemic states:
- A case can simultaneously contain:
  1. Record $e_1$: `source_type = 'PATIENT_REPORTED'`, `epistemic_status = 'KNOWN'`, value = *"Normotensive on pills"*
  2. Record $e_2$: `source_type = 'STAFF_ENTERED'`, `epistemic_status = 'KNOWN'`, value = *"195/115 mmHg"*
  3. Record $e_3$: `source_type = 'AI_INFERRED'`, `epistemic_status = 'INFERRED'`, value = *"Hypertensive Urgency with acute target organ risk"*
  4. Conflict $c_1$: `status = 'ACTIVE'`, linking $e_1$ and $e_2$.
  5. Record $e_4$: `source_type = 'CLINICIAN_VERIFIED'`, `epistemic_status = 'VERIFIED'`, value = *"Confirmed Stage 3 Hypertensive Crisis; admit to HDU"*, signed by Dr. Mohanty, MD.

This design guarantees that **historical truth is never erased**. The doctor sees what the patient stated, what the nurse measured, what the AI calculated, and what final clinical judgment was rendered.
